Definition

No chart is stored: the spindle/bulge meridian height is an incomplete elliptic integral of the second kind (numerical quadrature in the builder); the one elementary member, a = 1, is the sphere, already charted in charts.py. The shipped implementation is authoritative; an unverified transcription would silently define a different surface.
