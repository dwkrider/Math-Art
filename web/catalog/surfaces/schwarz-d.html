Schwarz D Surface

Schwarz D Surface is a triply periodic minimal surface, defined as a nodal algebraic surface, genus 3 per unit cell, embedded, with Pn-3m symmetry (a crystallographic space group).

About

Schwarz's diamond surface, the P surface's conjugate: the two are the same local geometry rotated through a right angle in the associate family, so a patch of one bends into a patch of the other without stretching. Its two labyrinths follow the bonds of the diamond lattice, which is where the name comes from.
