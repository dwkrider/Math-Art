Dini Surface

Dini Surface is a singly periodic surface of constant negative Gaussian curvature, given by a parametrisation, embedded, with a continuous symmetry group.

About

Take the pseudosphere and drag it along a helix as it turns: the result keeps the same constant negative curvature but winds around an axis, which is why it is often drawn as a twisted horn. Constant curvature survives the twisting because the motion is a screw, and a screw is a rigid motion.
