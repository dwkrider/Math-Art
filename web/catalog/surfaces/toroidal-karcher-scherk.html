Definition

Vertical handles added to Karcher-Scherk saddle towers with at least 6 ends, first mentioned in Karcher's Tokyo notes; the mirrored page shows 5-ended members with triangular-prism symmetry in a 1-parameter family of end angles. Distinct from the genus-1 Costa-Scherk tower record, which needs a half-period phase shift.
