Math Art

An interactive companion to the Math Art project. Each module explores one family of mathematical objects, drawn live in your browser from exact geometry — no pre-rendered pictures, and no computation anywhere but on your own machine.

Polyhedra

471 solids

The Platonic and Archimedean solids, the Catalan duals, all ninety-two Johnson solids, the Kepler–Poinsot stars, the uniform polyhedra and their duals, compounds, zonohedra, geodesic spheres and toroids, and the notable one-off solids — Dürer's, Jessen's, Schönhardt's, Klein's regular map — with their symmetry groups, exact metrics and notation.

Companion to the Math Art Blender extension. Source on GitHub.
