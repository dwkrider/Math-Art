Surfaces

A live triangle-mesh viewer for the surfaces the Math Art extension generates. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. The two panels below are linked: turning either turns both, which is how two reconstructions of the same surface are compared without a difference in viewpoint hiding a difference in shape. Front and back faces are shaded differently, because on an open surface that is the only way to read which way the sheet folds.

Instancing

A periodic surface's unit cell is one patch repeated under a symmetry group. Pass instances — a flat Float32Array of column-major 4×4 matrices — and the patch is uploaded once and drawn instanced, so a cell of two hundred copies costs one patch of geometry rather than two hundred. Reflected copies are handled: the shader flips the facing test by the sign of each instance's determinant, so a mirrored copy still shows its front face to the viewer.
