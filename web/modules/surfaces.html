Surfaces

Minimal surfaces, the triply-periodic families, algebraic and quadric surfaces, constant-curvature and constant-mean-curvature surfaces, and the classical named ones — each with the definition it is actually given by, and what is known about its curvature, topology and symmetry.

Definitions, curvature, topology and symmetry come from the project's surface database. Meshes are baked from the extension's own generators.
