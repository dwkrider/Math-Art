Polyhedra

Every solid here is drawn in your browser from its exact coordinates — nothing is a pre-rendered picture. Pick one from the catalogue to turn it over, switch how it is drawn, and read what is known about it.

Every one of the 471 solids also has its own page: see the full A–Z index.

Geometry, symmetry and metrics come from the project's polyhedron database. All computation happens locally in your browser.
